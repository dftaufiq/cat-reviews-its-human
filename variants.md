# Variants — pairwise internal judging

Judge criteria (lexicographic, per RULES.txt): (1) funny one-star cat review
of its human; (2) specific, recognisable complaint; (3) feline voice, human
measured by cat standards, reveals more about the cat; (4) tight + surprising,
turn/sting in the last few words, rewards a second read; (5) deadpan shape of
a real one-star review, absurd content. Plus: margin — pick the variant that
wins TELAK, not tipis (margin 1 = 0.6/call, margin 5 = 1.0/call).
Every variant MUST have 2-beat + reveal in the final clause; "di mana
twist-nya?" asked of each. 1-beat variants were discarded before the table.
